OCT 2026 · Universität Klagenfurt

Forty-eight hours, two briefs, one weekend on campus.

Teams of two to four pick a brief on Friday evening and ship something that runs by Sunday morning. Hardware, mentors and food are on site; whatever you build stays yours.

Open to every student in the area — first semester or last. Applying solo is fine, we match you into a team.
